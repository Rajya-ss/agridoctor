About

AgriDoctor AI looks at a photo of a plant leaf and names the most likely disease. The model is stored on this phone, so it works without internet after the first visit.

The result is an AI estimate from one photo. It is not a laboratory diagnosis. Before using any spray or chemical, show the plant to a local agriculture officer or Krishi Vigyan Kendra.
